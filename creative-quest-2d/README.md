# Creative Quest 2D — v0.4
100 unique levels across 10 worlds, 10 Boss battles. Original first 30 levels retained and 70 more added. Pixel-art character and guardian sprite animations are embedded in index.html. Different biome rendering for each world, joystick/tap movement, skill tree, XP and local progress persistence. Seven challenge formats: select, evidence, classify, pair, write, repair, forge. No paid API. All training cases are invented. Open-ended answers are checked by a human self-review rubric or optionally discussed in ChatGPT; automatic grading of arbitrary phrasing is NOT claimed. Boss completion is manually confirmed. Remaining 400 levels are not ready. Full separate editable source files and PNG sprite sheets are in the downloadable v0.4 ZIP.
