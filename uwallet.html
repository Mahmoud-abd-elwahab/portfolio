Uwallet Campaign Studies

The assignment

For the Back to School direction, make a payment bracelet feel like a natural part of a child’s school routine, balancing independence with parental visibility.

Project context
Self-initiated exploration; not presented as a commissioned or launched campaign
My role
Campaign art direction exploration
Scope
Visual development

Visual approach

Use one repeatable gesture: the child points to the bracelet when it is time to pay. The gesture carries the idea from home into school-day situations.

My contributionDeveloped the Back to School art direction as a self-initiated exploration of an existing brand brief. The entertainment visual is presented as a separate direction, not as part of one commissioned rollout.
