The assignment

Explore visual directions for digital entertainment and school spending. The Back to School brief focuses on making a payment bracelet part of a child’s routine, balancing independence with parental visibility.

Project context
leoden dentsu agency work + personal concept studies
My role
Visual development / Back to School art direction
Scope
Campaign visual development

Visual approach

The brief frames a practical tension: children need spending independence while parents want visibility. The visual response makes the bracelet part of a familiar routine. A child points to it when it is time to pay, turning the product into a repeatable visual cue.

My contributionVisual development across agency work and personal explorations. The independent Back to School direction develops a bracelet-pointing gesture across school-day situations.
